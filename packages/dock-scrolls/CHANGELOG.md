## 0.16.0

### Minor Changes

- A second theme for the dock: Scrolls (a wizard's desk), in the new module
  `modular-av-dock-scrolls`. It works with the dock at 0.16.0.

  - Each camera is a picture in a scroll of worn vellum with a roll at each
    side, on a desk of dark wood seen from above. Docked, the scrolls lie a
    little askew and apart, as if laid down by hand.
  - Four cut stones in the player's colour hold each scroll open. They glow
    while the player speaks, go dull while they're muted, and are cracked
    and colourless when the player is out of the call.
  - A row of pen-drawn signs under the picture lights blue with a voice; on
    your own scroll it lights as far as your level.
  - Each name is cut into a plate of old bone on the scroll, and the
    states' icons hang on the scroll's left rod.
  - The header and the voice panel are papers written in ink, the buttons
    are cut stones, and where the cameras leave room a mortar, a vial,
    crystals, coins, a key and, with a lot of room, a wand lie on the desk.
  - Menus and the settings window take a palette of indigo, cream and gold.

  The pictures of things are rendered with Blender from scenes kept in the
  repository as scripts; the three photographed textures they use are CC0
  and named in the module's notices. A scroll takes room: cameras that fill
  the dock are somewhat smaller than in the dock's own look.
