# Third-party notices: modular-av-dock-scrolls

This module bundles no third-party code. Its artwork (`art/`) was made
for this module and is under its MIT licence: the drawn pictures (SVG) by
a script in its repository, and the rendered ones (WebP) by Blender from
scenes that are scripts in the same place (`tools/art/scrolls`). No image
model and no outside artwork went into them.

The rendered pictures show three photographed surfaces, used as textures
in those scenes. All three are published under CC0 1.0 (public domain),
which asks for no credit; they are named here so that the pictures can be
traced and made again:

- `dark_wood` (the desk, the wand) and `smoked_walnut_veneer` (the
  scrolls' rods), from Poly Haven: <https://polyhaven.com/a/dark_wood>,
  <https://polyhaven.com/a/smoked_walnut_veneer>
- `Paper006` (the vellum of the scrolls and the papers), from ambientCG:
  <https://ambientcg.com/a/Paper006>

The texture files themselves are not part of this module or of its
repository: the render script downloads them, pinned by their hashes.
