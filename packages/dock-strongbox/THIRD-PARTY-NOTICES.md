# Third-party notices: modular-av-dock-strongbox

This module bundles no third-party code. Its artwork (`art/`) was made
for this module and is under its MIT licence: the pictures (WebP) are
rendered by Blender from scenes that are scripts in its repository
(`tools/art/strongbox`). Every shape in them is built by those scripts.
No image model went into them.

The pictures show four things that were not made here. All four are
published under CC0 1.0 (public domain), which asks for no credit; they
are named here so that the pictures can be traced and made again.

Four photographed surfaces, used as textures, from Poly Haven:

- `dark_wooden_planks` (the box's boards, the split grain where the
  knife went in, and the wear of the stencilled names):
  <https://polyhaven.com/a/dark_wooden_planks>
- `rust_coarse_01` (the iron of everything: its patches and pitting,
  without its colour): <https://polyhaven.com/a/rust_coarse_01>
- `poly_wool_herringbone` (the rag in a deafened keyhole, greyed):
  <https://polyhaven.com/a/poly_wool_herringbone>
- `velour_velvet` (the box's padded lining, greyed and tinted green):
  <https://polyhaven.com/a/velour_velvet>

None of these files is part of this module or of its repository: the
render script downloads them, pinned by their hashes.
