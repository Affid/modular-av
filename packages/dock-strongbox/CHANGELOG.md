# @modular-av/dock-strongbox

## 0.16.0

### Minor Changes

- A new theme for the dock: Strongbox (a rogue's locked box), in the new
  module `modular-av-dock-strongbox`. It works with the dock at 0.16.0.

  - The dock is the front of a box of dark oiled boards in a forged iron
    band, with folded iron brackets on its corners and a padlock at the
    header's right.
  - Each camera is a window bound in an iron strap with a lock over its
    top edge; three kinds of lock take turns so that neighbours differ.
  - A voice lights the lamp inside the box, amber through the keyhole,
    and on a poor link the lamp gutters. Muted, an iron shutter slides
    down and a padlock hangs on the hasp; deafened, a rag fills the
    keyhole; out of the call, the lock is forced. A camera that is off
    shows the box's padded velvet lining.
  - Your level is the lamp's flame through a slot in the strap under your
    own camera.
  - Each name is stencilled in pale paint on the boards; states and badges
    stand on iron plates; the buttons are forged plates, a pressed one
    sunk in its rim.
  - Where the cameras leave room, a throwing knife, a ring of lockpicks, a
    ring of keys and a coil of rope with a grappling hook come onto the
    bare boards.

  The pictures are rendered with Blender from scenes kept in the
  repository as scripts. The four photographed textures they use are CC0
  and named in the module's notices. A window takes room: cameras that
  fill the dock are somewhat smaller than in the dock's own look.
