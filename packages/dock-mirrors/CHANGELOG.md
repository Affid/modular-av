# @modular-av/dock-mirrors

## 0.16.0

### Minor Changes

- A fifth theme for the dock: Mirrors (a warlock's black mirror), in the
  new module `modular-av-dock-mirrors`. It works with the dock at 0.16.0.

  - The whole dock is one pane of old black glass in a worn, tarnished
    silver frame.
  - Spirit hands reach out of the glass and hold each camera by its
    sides, in two poses laid so that neighbours differ.
  - A voice is a violet light welling up in the glass where the hands
    come out. A muted mirror fogs with breath; a deafened player's hands
    let their grip go slack; out of the call the mirror is smoked and
    cracked and the hands let go. A camera that is off is black glass
    reflecting a window.
  - Nine marks cut into the glass above your own camera light with your
    voice.
  - Each name is lettered on the glass, the player's colour a gem in a
    silver collet before it; states and badges are chips of glass in
    silver bezels.
  - The header and the voice panel are panes of the same mirror; the
    buttons are little panes in silver bezels, lit ember red or violet
    when pressed.
  - Where the cameras leave room, a hand holds up a contract, and from
    more room a second offers the quill.

  The pictures are rendered with Blender from scenes kept in the
  repository as scripts; the hands are MakeHuman's CC0 base mesh. The
  textures and the mesh are CC0 and named in the module's notices.
