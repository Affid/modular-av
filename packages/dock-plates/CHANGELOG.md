## 0.16.0

### Minor Changes

- A fourth theme for the dock: Plates (a fighter's harness), in the new
  module `modular-av-dock-plates`. It works with the dock at 0.16.0.

  - Each camera sits in a plate of dark, worn steel: hammered, dented,
    rusty in its corners, with a rolled edge and a rib round the picture.
    Docked, the plates lie on mail over deep blue cloth.
  - A cord in the player's colour is laced round each plate.
  - A voice polishes the plate. A muted plate goes dull and dark, and one
    whose player is out of the call rusts from its corners, loses a rivet
    and loses its cord down one side.
  - A knife lies under your own name, and only under yours: its blade
    shines as far as your level.
  - Each name is cut into a band of the plate left black from the forge,
    and the states' icons stand at the plate's upper left corner. Icons
    and badges shrink with a small camera.
  - The header and the voice panel are larger plates, the buttons small
    hammered ones: dark steel at rest, blued for a camera that is on, red
    paint for muted and for leaving.
  - Where the cameras leave room one of the fighter's things shows, the
    tallest that fits whole: a mace, a pair of gauntlets or a buckler.
  - Menus and the settings window take a palette of dark steel.

  The pictures are rendered with Blender from scenes kept in the
  repository as scripts. The three photographed textures they use are CC0
  and named in the module's notices. A plate takes room: cameras that
  fill the dock are somewhat smaller than in the dock's own look.
