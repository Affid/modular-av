# Third-party notices: modular-av-dock-plates

This module bundles no third-party code. Its artwork (`art/`) was made
for this module and is under its MIT licence: the pictures (WebP) are
rendered by Blender from scenes that are scripts in its repository
(`tools/art/plates`). Every shape in them is built by those scripts. No
image model went into them.

The pictures show three things that were not made here. All three are
published under CC0 1.0 (public domain), which asks for no credit; they
are named here so that the pictures can be traced and made again.

Three photographed surfaces, used as textures, from Poly Haven:

- `rusty_metal_sheet` (the steel of the plates, the mail, the buttons
  and the things: its patches, its pitting and its rust):
  <https://polyhaven.com/a/rusty_metal_sheet>
- `rough_linen` (the cord round a plate and the cloth under the mail,
  without its colour): <https://polyhaven.com/a/rough_linen>
- `fabric_leather_01` (straps, thongs and grips):
  <https://polyhaven.com/a/fabric_leather_01>

None of these files is part of this module or of its repository: the
render script downloads them, pinned by their hashes.
