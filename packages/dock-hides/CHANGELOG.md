# @modular-av/dock-hides

## 0.16.0

### Minor Changes

- A third theme for the dock: Hides (a barbarian's hall), in the new
  module `modular-av-dock-hides`. It works with the dock at 0.16.0.

  - Each camera is a picture on a hide, flesh side out, laced with thongs
    into a frame of four lashed poles; the hide's hair shows past its
    edge. Docked, the frames hang on a wall of dark upright timbers.
  - War paint in the player's colour is daubed round each picture from
    its corners.
  - Firelight shows through a hide while its player speaks. The hide goes
    dark and dry while they're muted, and when they are out of the call
    the thongs of one corner are cut and hang, and the paint is all but
    gone.
  - Tally marks under your own name fill with your level. No other hide
    has them.
  - Each name is branded into a tag of pale rawhide stitched to the hide,
    and the states' icons hang on the frame's left pole.
  - The header and the voice panel are pieces of hide nailed to the wall,
    the buttons are the heads of hand-forged iron nails, and where the
    cameras leave room a horned skull hangs from a peg.
  - Menus and the settings window take a palette of smoke, tallow and
    firelight.

  The pictures are rendered with Blender from scenes kept in the
  repository as scripts. The four photographed textures they use and the
  scanned skull are CC0 and named in the module's notices. A frame takes
  room: cameras that fill the dock are somewhat smaller than in the
  dock's own look.
