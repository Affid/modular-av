# Third-party notices: modular-av-dock-hides

This module bundles no third-party code. Its artwork (`art/`) was made
for this module and is under its MIT licence: the rendered pictures
(WebP) by Blender from scenes that are scripts in its repository
(`tools/art/hides`), and the drawn one (SVG) by a script in the same
place. No image model went into them.

The rendered pictures show six things that were not made here. All six
are published under CC0 1.0 (public domain), which asks for no credit;
they are named here so that the pictures can be traced and made again.

Five photographed surfaces, used as textures, from Poly Haven:

- `fabric_leather_02` (the hides, the name's tag, the nailed pieces):
  <https://polyhaven.com/a/fabric_leather_02>
- `fabric_leather_01` (the thongs):
  <https://polyhaven.com/a/fabric_leather_01>
- `rough_wood` (the poles and the pegs):
  <https://polyhaven.com/a/rough_wood>
- `rusty_metal_04` (the iron of the buttons, without its colour):
  <https://polyhaven.com/a/rusty_metal_04>
- `wood_trunk_wall` (the wall of the hall):
  <https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_trunk_wall>

One scanned shape, the skull that hangs on the wall:

- "White-tailed Deer: Skull and Mandible", a 3D scan of a cast in the
  Education and Outreach collection of the Smithsonian's National Museum
  of Natural History (record `edanmdm:nmnheducation_15006160`), which the
  Smithsonian's Open Access catalogue marks CC0:
  <https://3d.si.edu/object/3d/white-tailed-deer-skull-and-mandible:fcc69841-d8be-4103-be0f-143e374c9ce6>.
  The skull is shown stained and aged, with horns that were made here: a
  deer carries antlers, so the beast on the wall is an invented one.

None of these files is part of this module or of its repository: the
render script downloads them, pinned by their hashes.
