# @modular-av/dock-spaceship

## 0.16.0

### Minor Changes

- A new theme for the dock: Spaceship (a cargo hauler's hold), in the new
  module `modular-av-dock-spaceship`. It works with the dock at 0.16.0.

  - The dock is a bulkhead of grey-green painted steel sheets, riveted,
    edged in angle steel; the header and the voice panel are gunmetal
    plates riveted on, with a pilot lamp for the call.
  - Each camera is a small CRT monitor in a chipped yellow steel case,
    behind curved dusty glass, the tube's scan lines in its margin.
  - Each name is lit on a green 14-segment readout in the monitor's chin,
    and slides along it when it is long.
  - A voice drives the readout full and lights the TX lamp. Muted, gaffer
    tape crosses the grille; deafened, the headset hangs on the case; a
    weak link snows; out of the call the set is off. A camera that is
    off shows a dim raster; the player's colour is a strip of tape on the
    case's corner.
  - Your level is a column of green segments in your own monitor's side.
  - The buttons are rubber keys, those that latch with an LED lit when
    the key is down.
  - Where the cameras leave room, a roll of tape, a wrench, a clipboard
    and a fire extinguisher hang on tool rails on the bulkhead.

  The pictures are rendered with Blender from scenes kept in the
  repository as scripts. The photographed textures and scanned models
  they use are CC0 and named in the module's notices; the readout's font
  is DSEG14 Classic, under the SIL Open Font License. A monitor's case
  takes room: cameras that fill the dock are somewhat smaller than in the
  dock's own look.
