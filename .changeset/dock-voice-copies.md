The dock shows who receives voices (#28). A player whose browser runs a
module receiving voices gets an ear on their camera's tile and in the
player list; its tooltip names each module and whose voices it asked for,
and who declined or hasn't answered yet. While a module asks for your
voice, a line in the voice panel says so ("Transcriber (Gamemaster) asks
for your voice"); it opens the list of those modules, each with a switch
that allows or refuses it there, and the core's choice and "Forget my
answers". In the settings window, another module's choice now sits in
the window's two columns, and its buttons take their own width.
