The dock shows who receives voices (#28). A player whose browser runs a
module receiving voices gets an ear on their camera's tile and in the
player list, saying whom each module receives, who refused and who
hasn't answered. While a module asks for your voice, a line in the voice
panel says so ("Transcriber wants your voice"), standing out until you
answer; only the module's title is cut short. It opens the list of those
modules, unanswered first, each with where it runs, what it receives,
and an Allow | Refuse pair showing your answer; below, what modules you
haven't answered get, with a button to the settings. In the settings
window, another module's choice now sits in the window's two columns,
and its buttons take their own width. The dock's budgets go to 92,000
bytes of script and 41,000 of styles.
