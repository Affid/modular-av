Milestone 2: voice and video at the table through Foundry's own camera dock.
The core joins calls with tokens a GM's browser signs, plays audio itself,
applies mute in every connection state, reconnects with backoff, lets the
newest tab win, enforces GM blocks on receivers and shows a plain status.
`modular-av-livekit` carries calls over LiveKit (self-hosted or Cloud).
