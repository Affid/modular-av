The vines theme is redrawn. Each camera now sits in a frame of woven
branches with ivy growing from one of its corners, and no two cameras
side by side have the same ivy. A muted player's leaves turn to autumn,
a player who is out of the call has bare stems, and the picture's edge
glows in a light tint of the speaker's colour in place of the speaking
ring. The dock, its menus and the settings window take a palette of bark
and leaf instead of the green one. The frames reach a little further
past each camera than before (11% of its width, up from 8%), so docked
cameras are slightly smaller.
