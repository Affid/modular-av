The vines theme is redrawn. Each camera now sits in a frame that looks
grown from branches, twisted into an uneven loop with ivy from one of its
corners. There are four frames, no two the same shape, and the picture is
cut to its frame, so cameras are no longer rectangles. A muted player's
leaves turn to autumn, a player who is out of the call has bare stems,
and a soft light in a tint of the speaker's colour shows on the picture's
rim in place of the speaking ring. The dock, its menus and the settings
window take a palette of bark and leaf instead of the green one. The
frames reach a little further past each camera than before (11% of its
width, up from 8%), so docked cameras are slightly smaller.
