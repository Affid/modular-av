The vines theme is redrawn. Scattered, each camera sits in a frame that
looks grown from branches, twisted into an uneven loop with ivy from one
of its corners: four frames, no two the same shape, with the picture cut
to its frame, so cameras are no longer rectangles. Docked, floating or in
a window, the cameras sit in the openings of one lattice of the same
branches, and neighbours share the branch between them. A muted player's
leaves turn to autumn, a player who is out of the call has bare stems,
and a soft light in a tint of the speaker's colour shows on the picture's
rim in place of the speaking ring. The dock, its menus and the settings
window take a palette of bark and leaf instead of the green one.
