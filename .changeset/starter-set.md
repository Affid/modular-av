New: the starter set (`modular-av-starter-set`), voice and video in one
install. It requires the core, the dock, LiveKit and noise cancellation
and recommends the camera background, so Foundry installs and enables
them together (the camera background stays optional). It holds no code
of its own and isn't listed on the "Plan your modules" board.
