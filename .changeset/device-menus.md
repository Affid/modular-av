The microphone and the camera have menus: a small arrow beside each
button (which still mutes, or turns the camera on and off) opens them.
The microphone's has the microphone and speakers, the input mode, the
input sensitivity while voice activity is on (the call's own level bar,
applied at once) and noise cancellation; the camera's has the camera and
its background. The sliders button no longer duplicates them: it is
"More controls", there only while another module adds quick controls of
its own (it shared its name with the settings window's button before).
